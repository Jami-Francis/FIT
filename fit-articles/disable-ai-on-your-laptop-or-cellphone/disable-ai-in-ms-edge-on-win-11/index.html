Disable AI in Edge on Win 11

This guide provides targeted instructions for disabling artificial intelligence features, background machine learning modules, telemetry assistants, and cloud recommendation engines specific to Microsoft Edge on Windows 11. Follow the steps below to restore complete user control and enhance system resource efficiency.

Step-by-Step Deactivation Procedure

Step 1: Open Edge Settings: Open Edge, tap the three dot menu ( ⋮ ) and select 'Settings'.

Step 2: Disable Copilot Sidebar: Navigate to 'Sidebar' or 'Copilot and Copilot Pro' settings and toggle off 'Show Copilot'.

Step 3: Disable Discover & Compose Features: Go to Settings > System and performance, and turn off AI enhancements like 'Compose (AI-writing)' and 'Enhance images with AI'.

Step 4: Restart your device to implement the changes.
