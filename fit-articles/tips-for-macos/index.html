Critical Quick Checks

  • Network Down - Check if a website is truly down or if it's just your connection.
  • Scamwise - Verify if an online store is legitimate before purchasing.

Standard Productivity Apps

  • iCloud Drive - Keep files synced between your Mac, iPhone, and iPad.
  • TeamViewer - Securely connect to help family or friends with PC/Mac issues.
  • Zoom - Reliable video conferencing for personal and business meetings.
  • Evernote - Keep family projects or business notes organized.
  • Adobe Online PDF Tools - Quickly compress or convert PDF files.
  • Dropbox - Easy file sharing for families and small teams.

Productivity Tools

  • Rectangle - Easily snap windows to half-screen or corners.
  • CleanShot X - Professional-grade screen captures and annotations.
  • Bartender - Organize your crowded Mac menu bar.
  • Alfred - A powerful search tool to find apps, files, and website links faster.
  • OmniDiskSweeper - Find and delete large, unnecessary files to free up space.
  • Backblaze - Simple, automated offsite cloud backup for your Mac.
  • CopyClip - Simple clipboard manager to save frequently used text.
  • Clockify - Simple time tracking for small business or freelance work.
  • Todoist - Keep your daily home or business tasks organized.
