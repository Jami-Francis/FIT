USB Adapters

At Francis IT, we know how frustrating it can be when your old USB cables (mouse, printer, etc.) don't fit your new computer's USB-C ports. Or you might be in the opposite situation where you have a new USB-C SSD (Solid State Drive) that you cannot connect to your old computer. Either situation can be solved by purchasing the appropriate USB adapter or cable if one didn't come packaged with your new device. However, in the last example the newer drive may operate at a slower USB speed.

If you have a newer laptop with very few USB-C female ports, there are USB hubs (or spitters) which will allow you to connect multiple USB-C devices.

Either way, always check whether an adapter or cable supports data transfer and device charging.

Common Scenarios
  • Connecting a newer drive to an older PC: Requires a USB-A male to USB-C female adapter.
  • Connecting a traditional mouse to a new USB-C laptop: Requires a USB-C male to USB-A female adapter.
