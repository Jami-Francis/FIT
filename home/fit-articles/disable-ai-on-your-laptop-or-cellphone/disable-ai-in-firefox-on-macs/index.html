Disable AI in Firefox on Macs

This guide provides targeted instructions for disabling artificial intelligence features, background machine learning modules, telemetry assistants, and cloud recommendation engines specific to Mozilla Firefox on macOS. Follow the steps below to restore complete user control and enhance system resource efficiency.

Step-by-Step Deactivation Procedure

Step 1: Open Firefox Preferences: Open Firefox, type about:preferences in the address bar and press Enter.

Step 2: Disable Firefox Labs: Scroll down to the 'Firefox Labs' section and ensure all experimental AI summary and chat features are unchecked.

Step 3: Configure about:config: Navigate to about:config, accept the warning, search for browser.ml.enable, and double-click to set it to false.

Step 4: Restart your device to implement the changes.
