Disable AI in Chrome on Win 11

This guide provides targeted instructions for disabling artificial intelligence features, background machine learning modules, telemetry assistants, and cloud recommendation engines specific to Google Chrome on Windows 11. Follow the steps below to restore complete user control and enhance system resource efficiency.

Step-by-Step Deactivation Procedure

Step 1: Open Chrome Settings: Open Chrome, type chrome://settings in the Chrome address bar, and press Enter.

Step 2: Turn Off Experimental AI: Select 'Experimental AI' from the left menu and turn off all generative options.

Step 3: Disable Chrome Flags: Type chrome://flags in the Chrome address bar, search for 'Optimization Guide On Device Model', and change to 'Disabled'.

Step 4: Restart your device to implement the changes.
